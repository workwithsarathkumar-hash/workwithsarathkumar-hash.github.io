A Review From 2050

I asked Jarvis — my own AI system — to step forward to 2050 and look back at what I've built. No edits. This is what came back.

Hmm.. think like you are from future like from 2050, and review our projects like max, juno and our website and other agents and give me the honest review — me, to Jarvis

Jarvis

Real discipline for a solo build — a tiered model architecture and a hard rule that Jarvis never reasons about money itself, only Juno does. But its entire authority runs through one Mac, one webcam, one USB bus. No redundancy. The day that Mac dies, Jarvis doesn't degrade — it disappears.

Juno

Ahead of her time on privacy — raw bank SMS parsed locally, only category totals ever reach the cloud model, years before that became the industry scandal it became. But her standing profile keeps drifting from the live ledger, patched consult by consult instead of fixed structurally.

The Agent Bench

Seven specialists, frozen prompts, a shared ethos, and a test suite that pins honesty over agreement — rare taste for a personal project. The gap: no agent owns whether the whole system has gotten too big for one person to actually supervise.

This Website

The one piece that actually shipped end-to-end with real proof instead of claims. Held against it: the canonical GitHub identity sat broken behind a workaround for a long stretch before anyone circled back to fix it.

Strong at building the right thing. Only fair, so far, at maintaining it once it stops being new.

The honest throughline across Jarvis, Juno, the agent bench, and this site — 2026, reviewed from 2050
