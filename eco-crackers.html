ECO — A Cleaner Diwali

Burst The Joy. Not The Planet.

Pick a cracker, drag the stick to the fuse, and let it go — the full sound and spark, none of the smoke, none of the noise, none of the risk. Built end-to-end by Jarvis, my personal AI system. No build tools, no tracking, nothing stored. (Old enough to know Diwali's just another day on the calendar. Young enough to still want the bang — just not the smog, the burns, or the credit card bill.)

Drag the glowing stick onto the pulsing fuse — or just tap the fuse, or focus the stick and press Enter.

Why this exists

Every Diwali, the air over Indian cities spikes to some of the worst particulate levels of the year, burn wards fill up, and streets full of dogs, birds, and infants sit through hours of noise well past safe limits — all for a few seconds of color each. This page is the same spark, the same sound, the same small ritual of lighting something by hand — just moved entirely off the atmosphere and onto a screen. Pick a cracker, light it as many times as you want, and leave nothing behind.
